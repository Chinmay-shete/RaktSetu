🩸 Eligibility Screening
Quick health check — takes ~2 minutes
Question 1 of 9
Question 1 of 9
Are you between 18 and 65 years old?
Blood donors must be at least 18 and not older than 65 years per NBTC guidelines.
Question 2 of 9
Is your weight at least 45 kg?
Minimum weight requirement for safe blood donation is 45 kg (100 lbs).
Question 3 of 9
Have you had any of these: Diabetes, Heart disease, High blood pressure, or Thyroid problems?
Chronic illnesses may temporarily or permanently defer you from donation.
Question 4 of 9
Have you ever been diagnosed with HIV, Hepatitis B, or Hepatitis C?
These conditions permanently defer donors to protect recipients.
Question 5 of 9
Have you taken any antibiotics or blood-thinning medications in the last 7 days?
Certain medicines can affect blood safety. Temporary deferral may apply.
Question 6 of 9
Have you donated blood in the last 90 days?
Whole blood donors must wait at least 90 days between donations to allow blood recovery.
Question 7 of 9
Are you currently pregnant or have you delivered a baby in the last 6 months?
Pregnancy and 6 months post-delivery require deferral for maternal and fetal safety.
Question 8 of 9
Have you had any surgery, dental work, or tattoos in the last 3 months?
These procedures carry infection risk. Temporary deferral of 3-6 months may apply.
Question 9 of 9
Are you currently feeling well and not sick with cold, fever, or flu?
Acute illnesses require temporary deferral until recovery. You must be in good health to donate.
About This Screening
✓ This is a self-assessment only, not a medical diagnosis
